What this proposal solves
The 6 core problems this proposal solves
The first three are blockers — unsolved, the project either burns hardware or collects untrustworthy data. The last three decide whether the system can run long-term, safely, and reproducibly. Every item comes with a concrete verification method — nothing is concluded on "should be fine".
1Connecting the 18 V PV panel directly to EVK10941 destroys the chip instantly
NOWThe AEM10941 PV input limit is ~5 V①, while the delivered panel's Voc is ~21–22 V — over 4× the limit. Wired as originally assumed, the chip dies on the spot.
FIXSwitch to a CN3791 high-voltage MPPT charge path (input rating ~28 V①); EVK10941 leaves the main chain and becomes an independent "two harvesting paths compared" research branch.
VERIFYPower-on checklist: current-limited supply first, diode-mode check of reverse-polarity protection, insulation test between the 18 V and low-voltage zones.
2An 8 W-class switching supply will pollute µV-level ECG #1 technical risk
NOWAfter the panel change, switching power rose from 0.27 W to ~8 W (~30×). The envelope frequency of buck/boost converters in PFM burst mode can fall inside the 0.05–40 Hz ECG band; demodulated through electrode impedance it looks like physiology — data that "looks right but is wrong".
FIXTwo architectural measures that cut the coupling path instead of filtering: ① split topology — 18 V and the charger stay in the backpack unit; only <4.2 V reaches the chest; ② dual-battery alternation — one battery charges while the other powers the load, so during acquisition no switching supply is active on the supply rail.
VERIFYTC-05 ECG-phantom 2×2 factorial comparison, quantifying the 0.5–40 Hz noise-floor delta; the result decides whether dual batteries are actually needed.
3Can continuous ECG acquisition truly be self-powered
NOWUnder the v1.0 small-flex-panel assumption, self-powering reached only ~7%, forcing the sampling duty cycle down to 6.7% (40 s per 10 min) to break even.
FIXRe-budgeted with the 30 W panel: continuous acquisition is now feasible, with energy autonomy ratio EAR of 1.0–3.9 across lighting scenarios. Duty-cycling is demoted from "feasibility prerequisite" to "a saving measure for overcast days".
VERIFYTC-06 unattended 72-hour energy balance; TC-11 outdoor on-body measurement of EAR ≥ 1.5.
4Battery capacity cannot ride out consecutive overcast days
NOWThe existing 1000 mAh battery stores only 3.7 Wh — autonomy of 0.64 days. Two overcast days in a row means blackout, making long-term wear studies impossible.
FIXScale up to 2 × 2500 mAh, raising autonomy to 3.2 days — in line with the 3–5 day engineering convention for off-grid PV, and a natural fit for the dual-battery alternation architecture.
VERIFYTC-19 measured continuous run time after shading ≥ 2.5 days.
5Heat from the panel and electronics can injure skin
NOWUnder sun, the PV panel's rear face can reach 60–70 °C②. Following the common practice of placing a thermal pad between panel and body (TEUCER HP600 is a heat-conducting silicone pad) would pump that heat straight into the skin.
FIXSeparate the two thermal paths explicitly: conduction for "component → enclosure" cooling; insulation on the body side — a 10–20 mm air gap plus low-conductivity foam/aerogel pad.
VERIFYTC-07 in two stages: ex-vivo skin-phantom test first, on-body only after passing; 4-point synchronous thermometry, skin side < 41 °C③.
6Experimental data must be reproducible and traceable
NOWHost timestamps on the two independent radio links (ECG + power telemetry) suffer tens to hundreds of ms of Bluetooth buffering jitter, so "the light right now" cannot be matched to "the waveform right now"; configuration drift would make an anomalous curve untraceable three months later.
FIXHardware-level sync — the telemetry MCU pulses a BITalino digital input, embedding markers in the data stream itself; Run Manifest — every run records firmware commit, hardware version hash, calibrated battery capacity, and measured irradiance.
VERIFYTC-09 sync residual < 2 samples; CI enforces that the firmware SHA in each manifest really exists in the repo.
① To be confirmed against datasheets; ② to be measured; ③ IEC 60601-1 limit for applied parts in prolonged skin contact without heating — the exact tier depends on material and contact duration, check Tables 23/24. 1.2 The bottleneck moved: from "can't harvest enough" to "can't store enough"

Daily harvest is 14–22 Wh, while the existing 1000 mAh battery stores only 3.7 Wh. It fills early each day and the rest is discarded; the first overcast stretch then kills the system. The standard design variable for off-grid PV is autonomy (days), conventionally 3–5.

1.3 A 30 W panel driving a 0.24 W load is not waste

Panel capability (8–23 W) far exceeds what the charger can absorb (~5 W). This is standard off-grid practice — oversized array for low-light robustness:

  • Size for "just enough at sunny noon" → the system fails entirely in overcast, winter, or poor orientation;
  • Size for "enough even overcast" → on sunny days current is limited and surplus discarded, but the system works year-round.
  • This panel still outputs 3.5 W under overcast — enough to charge at full rate. Its value is not peak power; it is worst-case floor capability.

SECTION 02

System Architecture

Four layers. Hardware solves energy and noise; firmware solves scheduling and timestamps; software solves multi-source alignment and analysis; the data layer solves reproducibility.

2.1 Two decisions that shape the architecture

ADR-007 Split topology: no 18 V on the chest The system splits into two physical units. The 18 V power chain, MPPT charger, and batteries all stay in the backpack unit; only < 4.2 V reaches the chest unit over a 5-core shielded cable. Four problems solved at once:
① EMC — noise sources sit 0.5–1 m from the ECG amplifier with no common-mode path between; ② Safety — 18 V physically cannot approach the ECG electrodes; ③ Heat — all charger heat stays in the backpack; chest-unit dissipation < 1 mW; ④ Ergonomics — panel and battery weight rides in the backpack; the chest carries only tens of grams.
Cost one extra cable → a break-away design is mandatory (magnetic or frangible connector).
ADR-006 Dual-battery alternation: solve EMC by physical isolation, not filtering Two batteries: at any instant one is charging, the other is powering the load, roles swapped by the MCU every 30 min. During acquisition no switching supply is operating on the supply rail at all — noise is not filtered out, it simply does not exist in the measurement loop. The few-ms switchover gap is bridged by a supercapacitor: 5.4 V / 5 F stores 15.2 J, holding a 240 mW load for 63 s — a margin of over 10,000×.
Precondition a hardware interlock is mandatory to prevent accidental parallel connection of the batteries (a 0.5 V difference drives ~4.2 A of circulating current). This circuit must not see real batteries until TC-13 fully passes.
Deploy both together? Measure first, then decide the complexity ADR-006 is not cheap. Recommendation: in Phase 1 implement only the software-level "charger off during acquisition" (ADR-004), and quantify the noise with the TC-05 ECG-phantom comparison:
· if the noise-floor delta < 10 µVrms① → drop the second battery, halve hardware complexity;
· if above → build ADR-006 and re-measure.
Do not invest hardware effort in dual batteries before TC-05 returns a result.

2.2 Layer responsibilities and boundaries

L1Hardware layer — energy & noise
Harvest, convert, store, and distribute energy without polluting the biosignal. Key constraints: every power node must be meterable (a precondition for EAR); the 18 V zone and the low-voltage zone must be physically partitioned.
L2Firmware layer — scheduling & timestamps
Energy state machine, battery switching, coulomb counting, hardware sync pulses, BLE telemetry and offline logging. Key constraints: the state machine must contain no hardware calls so it can be unit-tested on a PC; on watchdog failure the load must default to OFF.
L3Software layer — alignment & analysis
Capture, time-align, assess signal quality, compute energy metrics, and visualize across three independent data sources. Key constraint: time-alignment error must be < 10 ms and provable (not assumed).
L4Data & ops layer — reproducibility
Raw data immutable, processing re-runnable, every run traceable to the firmware and hardware versions of its day. Do not skimp on this layer — when an anomalous curve shows up three months from now, it is the only thing that can save you.
① Engineering rule-of-thumb threshold; the formal report should cite the IEC 60601-2-25 quantified noise requirements for ECG equipment.
