SECTION 08 · PLAN

Schedule & Verification Gates

The plan pivots on weeks 5–6: the TC-05 ECG-phantom noise comparison decides the simple vs complex route — no dual-battery hardware before it reports. Assumptions: procurement ≤ 2 wk, ethics ≤ 6 wk, same-day PCBs. Procurement and ethics must both start in week 1. W12+ is Phase 3 (optional, pending Q3) and write-up.

W1
Component verification & baseline measurements
panel weight & laminate type, measured Voc/Isc, BITalino model + BT/BLE check, HeartBIT packing list, TC-01 measured power draw, place orders.
Gate: all ⚠ items cleared; TC-01 values replace every power estimate and EAR is recomputed.
W2
Repo, conventions & ethics application
Git repo + run-manifest convention, CI skeleton, PCB-equipment capability survey, ethics application submitted.
Gate: P0 orders placed; ethics filed (critical path — earlier is better).
W3–4
PMB-H in-house board & firmware v0.1
PCB fab and assembly, power-on checklist all green, firmware drivers + state machine, TC-02 panel I-V, TC-17 temp-coefficient calibration.
Gate: power-on checklist fully passed (current-limited first power-up, 18 V vs LV insulation test).
W5–6
TC-05 ECG-phantom noise comparison — decision point
2×2 factorial (phantom 0 / 1 mV × harvester OFF / ON), 5 × 5 min per cell; Welch PSD on the 0.5–40 Hz floor, watching for irradiance-tracking peaks. TC-04 chain efficiency in parallel.
Decision: Δ noise floor < 10 µVrms → drop dual batteries; otherwise build ADR-006 and re-measure.
W7
Converge the architecture per TC-05
if dual batteries needed: TC-13 interlock verification (16 gate combos + 4 fault injections, current-limited supplies only, no real batteries).
Gate: real batteries only after TC-13 fully passes.
W8
LIB board, umbilical & thermal safety
TC-15 supercap hold-up, TC-16 cable break-away, TC-07 stage 1: ex-vivo skin-phantom thermal test.
Gate: no on-body work until thermal stage 1 passes.
W9–10
System integration & long runs
TC-06 continuous 72 h energy balance (first run attended, segmented), TC-19 measured autonomy, TC-09 clock-drift characterization.
Gate: telemetry completeness > 99%; EAR ≥ 1.5.
W11
On-body validation & dual-unit comparison
TC-07 stage 2 (gradual on-body; abort on score <4 or skin ≥40 °C), TC-08 dual-unit agreement, TC-11 outdoor EAR.
Gate: continuous-acquisition EAR ≥ 1.5; R-peak agreement > 99%.
