What this proposal solves
The 6 core problems this proposal solves
The first three are blockers — unsolved, the project either burns hardware or collects untrustworthy data. The last three decide whether the system can run long-term, safely, and reproducibly. Every item comes with a concrete verification method — nothing is concluded on "should be fine".
1Connecting the 18 V PV panel directly to EVK10941 destroys the chip instantly
NOWThe AEM10941 PV input limit is ~5 V①, while the delivered panel's Voc is ~21–22 V — over 4× the limit. Wired as originally assumed, the chip dies on the spot.
FIXSwitch to a CN3791 high-voltage MPPT charge path (input rating ~28 V①); EVK10941 leaves the main chain and becomes an independent "two harvesting paths compared" research branch.
VERIFYPower-on checklist: current-limited supply first, diode-mode check of reverse-polarity protection, insulation test between the 18 V and low-voltage zones.
2An 8 W-class switching supply will pollute µV-level ECG　#1 technical risk
NOWAfter the panel change, switching power rose from 0.27 W to ~8 W (~30×). The envelope frequency of buck/boost converters in PFM burst mode can fall inside the 0.05–40 Hz ECG band; demodulated through electrode impedance it looks like physiology — data that "looks right but is wrong".
FIXTwo architectural measures that cut the coupling path instead of filtering: ① split topology — 18 V and the charger stay in the backpack unit; only <4.2 V reaches the chest; ② dual-battery alternation — one battery charges while the other powers the load, so during acquisition no switching supply is active on the supply rail.
VERIFYTC-05 ECG-phantom 2×2 factorial comparison, quantifying the 0.5–40 Hz noise-floor delta; the result decides whether dual batteries are actually needed.
3Can continuous ECG acquisition truly be self-powered
NOWUnder the v1.0 small-flex-panel assumption, self-powering reached only ~7%, forcing the sampling duty cycle down to 6.7% (40 s per 10 min) to break even.
FIXRe-budgeted with the 30 W panel: continuous acquisition is now feasible, with energy autonomy ratio EAR of 1.0–3.9 across lighting scenarios. Duty-cycling is demoted from "feasibility prerequisite" to "a saving measure for overcast days".
VERIFYTC-06 unattended 72-hour energy balance; TC-11 outdoor on-body measurement of EAR ≥ 1.5.
4Battery capacity cannot ride out consecutive overcast days
NOWThe existing 1000 mAh battery stores only 3.7 Wh — autonomy of 0.64 days. Two overcast days in a row means blackout, making long-term wear studies impossible.
FIXScale up to 2 × 2500 mAh, raising autonomy to 3.2 days — in line with the 3–5 day engineering convention for off-grid PV, and a natural fit for the dual-battery alternation architecture.
VERIFYTC-19 measured continuous run time after shading ≥ 2.5 days.
5Heat from the panel and electronics can injure skin
NOWUnder sun, the PV panel's rear face can reach 60–70 °C②. Following the common practice of placing a thermal pad between panel and body (TEUCER HP600 is a heat-conducting silicone pad) would pump that heat straight into the skin.
FIXSeparate the two thermal paths explicitly: conduction for "component → enclosure" cooling; insulation on the body side — a 10–20 mm air gap plus low-conductivity foam/aerogel pad.
VERIFYTC-07 in two stages: ex-vivo skin-phantom test first, on-body only after passing; 4-point synchronous thermometry, skin side < 41 °C③.
6Experimental data must be reproducible and traceable
NOWHost timestamps on the two independent radio links (ECG + power telemetry) suffer tens to hundreds of ms of Bluetooth buffering jitter, so "the light right now" cannot be matched to "the waveform right now"; configuration drift would make an anomalous curve untraceable three months later.
FIXHardware-level sync — the telemetry MCU pulses a BITalino digital input, embedding markers in the data stream itself; Run Manifest — every run records firmware commit, hardware version hash, calibrated battery capacity, and measured irradiance.
VERIFYTC-09 sync residual < 2 samples; CI enforces that the firmware SHA in each manifest really exists in the repo.
① To be confirmed against datasheets; ② to be measured; ③ IEC 60601-1 limit for applied parts in prolonged skin contact without heating — the exact tier depends on material and contact duration, check Tables 23/24.
