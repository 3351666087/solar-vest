The lab has PCB fabrication equipment, compressing the iteration loop from "5–7 day fab" to same day. Its capability must be characterized first (Q7, §8):

Good news: the QFN problem is gone v1.0's worst process obstacle was the AEM10941's QFN-40 (0.5 mm pitch, bottom pad — impossible for a lab iron). After the change, AEM10941 is off the main chain; the hardest remaining package is the INA226's VSSOP-10, worked around with a ready-made module in scenario A. In all three scenarios, Rev B needs no outsourced PCBA.

6.4 Hand-soldering work list

Once the PCBs exist, the iron work (~1 h total, all easy through-hole):

  • J1 screw terminal / XT30 (PV input) ★☆☆
  • J2 / J3 JST-PH 2.0 vertical (batteries A/B) ★☆☆
  • J4 / J5 2-pin headers (NTC ×2) ★☆☆
  • J6 5-core connector (umbilical) ★☆☆
  • C10 / C11 supercaps ×2 (through-hole, mind polarity) ★★☆
  • XIAO nRF52840 module (castellated) ★★☆
  • test-point headers ×6 ★☆☆
  • J7 JST-PH (→ BITalino, measure polarity first) ★☆☆
The two jobs most likely to cause irreversible damage ① Lithium-cell lead termination — never solder on the cell body; handle one wire at a time, heat-shrink first, finish and insulate the red wire before touching the black; clear the bench of metal debris; keep a fireproof container handy.
② BITalino battery-lead polarity check — JST-PH has no keying; the two wires can be swapped in the housing and pin order varies by vendor. Measure polarity with the supplied battery first, photograph for the record, double-check with a second person; ⚠ whether BITalino has reverse-polarity protection is unknown — treat it as unprotected.

SECTION 08 · PLAN

Schedule & Verification Gates

The plan pivots on weeks 5–6: the TC-05 ECG-phantom noise comparison decides whether the rest follows the simple or the complex route. Do not invest in dual-battery hardware work before it reports.
Timeline assumptions: procurement lead time ≤ 2 weeks, ethics approval ≤ 6 weeks, lab-made PCBs available the same day; if any of these slips, the whole schedule shifts accordingly. W1–W2 have the highest parallelism, and procurement and the ethics application must both start in week 1. From W12 on: Phase 3 (optional, depending on the answer to Q3) and write-up of results.

W1
Component verification & baseline measurements
weigh the panel and confirm its laminate type, measure Voc/Isc, verify the BITalino model and BT/BLE version, check the HeartBIT packing list, TC-01 measured power draw, send out purchase orders.
Gate: all ⚠ items cleared; TC-01 measured values replace every power estimate in this proposal, and EAR is recomputed.
W2
Repo, conventions & ethics application
Git repo + run-manifest convention, CI skeleton, PCB-equipment capability survey, ethics application submitted.
Gate: P0 purchases ordered; ethics application submitted (this is the critical path — the earlier the better).
W3–4
PMB-H in-house board & firmware v0.1
PCB fabrication and assembly, power-on checks passed item by item, firmware drivers + state machine, TC-02 panel I-V, TC-17 temp-coefficient calibration.
Gate: power-on checklist fully passed (including first power-up on a current-limited supply and an insulation test between the 18 V zone and the low-voltage zone).
W5–6
TC-05 ECG-phantom noise comparison — decision point
2×2 factorial (phantom 0 / 1 mV × harvester OFF / ON), 5 runs × 5 min per cell; compute the 0.5–40 Hz noise floor with Welch's method and check whether the PSD shows spectral peaks that move with illumination. TC-04 harvesting-chain efficiency is completed in the same period.
Decision: Δ noise floor < 10 µVrms → drop dual batteries, saving half the hardware complexity; otherwise adopt ADR-006 and re-measure.
W7
Converge the architecture per TC-05
if dual batteries are needed: TC-13 interlock verification (16 gate combos + 4 fault injections, current-limited supplies only, no real batteries).
Gate: real batteries may be connected only after TC-13 passes in full.
W8
LIB board, umbilical & thermal safety
TC-15 supercap hold-up time, TC-16 cable pull-out safety, TC-07 stage 1: ex-vivo skin-phantom thermal test.
Gate: if thermal-safety stage 1 does not pass, nothing goes on-body.
W9–10
System integration & long runs
TC-06 continuous 72 h energy balance (first run attended, segmented), TC-19 measured autonomy, TC-09 clock-drift characterization.
Gate: telemetry completeness > 99%; EAR ≥ 1.5.
W11
On-body validation & dual-unit comparison
TC-07 stage 2 (gradual on-body exposure; stop immediately on a subjective score ≥4 or skin-side temperature ≥40 °C), TC-08 dual-unit agreement, TC-11 outdoor EAR.
Gate: continuous-acquisition EAR ≥ 1.5; R-peak agreement > 99%.
