## 1. How the three BITalino boards are assigned (new ADR-011)

The three boards identified in the photos:

| Code | What it is | Wireless | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | BITalino (r)evolution **Core BT**: green HC-05 module, part of the HeartBIT kit | **Classic Bluetooth** | Inside the HeartBIT enclosure (white base + red lid, shown opened in the photo), connected to its own 700 mAh battery |
| **B** | BITalino (r)evolution **Core BLE/BT**: blue module | **BLE** (Classic Bluetooth compatible) | Bare board |
| **C** | BITalino (r)evolution **Board BLE/BT**: full board, all sensors attached on the board | **BLE** (Classic Bluetooth compatible) | Full board |

**Assignment:**

| Role | Board | Power | Sensor | Data destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Device under test (DUT)**: solar-powered, worn on the body | **B** (Core BLE) | PMB-H → LIB (chest unit) | HeartBIT ECG sensor (UC-E6 plug) | Phone app (BLE), **works on both iPhone and Android** |
| **Signal-quality control**: battery only, worn at the same time | **C** (Board BLE) | Existing 523450 1000 mAh | ECG sensor on the board | Phone or computer (BLE) |
| **Reference / power comparison / spare** | **A** (Core BT, HeartBIT) | Its own 700 mAh | PulseSensor (PPG), as an independent heart-rate reference | Computer or Android (Classic Bluetooth, via the Bluetooth adapter included in the kit) |

**Why this assignment:**

1. **B and C are both BLE.** The only difference between the two units is the power path, so the comparison "effect of the solar harvester on ECG quality" is not confounded by a difference in wireless mode. Comparing A (Classic Bluetooth) against B would make the wireless mode itself a confounding variable.
2. **iPhone does not allow ordinary apps to use the Classic Bluetooth serial profile.** So the phone app only talks to the BLE boards B and C; A goes through a computer.
3. **A stays as the original HeartBIT** and doubles as the spare. If one of the three boards fails, the experiments do not stop.

⚠ **Note one limitation**: C is a full board with all its sensors permanently powered, so it draws more power than B. **C can only serve as the "signal quality" control, not as the "runtime" control.** Runtime gain (TC-24) uses B only, measured twice: once with the panel covered and once with normal wearing. This was already the main plan in v3.

## 3. Measured panel parameters

There is **no nameplate** on the back of the panel. All figures below come from your photos: a bank card and a tape measure were used as scale references, cross-checked by two methods.

| Item | Value | How it was obtained |
|---|---|---|
| Outline | **261.8 × 171 mm**, ±2 mm | Length measured from the tape-measure photo; width converted from the aspect ratio 1.531 in two photos (the two photos differ by 0.6%). Using the bank card as scale and correcting for the magnification caused by the panel's thickness, the width comes out at 170.5 mm, consistent with the above |
| Frame thickness | About **15 mm**, ±2 mm | Tape-measure photo |
| Construction | Aluminium frame, glass front (judged from the reflection texture), white backsheet | Photos |
| Junction box | About 38 × 48 mm, centred left-right, **does not protrude beyond the frame** | Back and side photos |
| Cells | **12 cells, 2 columns × 6 rows in series**, each about 67 × 32 mm, active area about 262 cm² | Automatic segmentation of the front photo |
| Cable | About 5 m, with a waterproof 2-pin connector, ending in two bare wires, red and blue | Photos |

**Electrical parameters inferred from the cells** (estimates; TC-02 will give the true values when the I-V curve is measured):

| Parameter | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| V<sub>oc</sub> | 7.7–8.2 V at 25 °C, **up to about 9 V in cold winter conditions** | 12 cells × 0.64–0.68 V per cell, V<sub>oc</sub> temperature coefficient about −0.3 %/°C, i.e. +0.3 % per °C colder |
| V<sub>mp</sub> | About 6.4–6.7 V at 25 °C, about 6.1 V when heated to 45 °C | 12 cells × 0.53–0.56 V |
| I<sub>sc</sub> | **0.8–0.9 A** | 21.8 cm² per cell × 37–41 mA/cm² |
| P<sub>max</sub> | **About 5–6 W** | 262 cm² × 1000 W/m² × cell efficiency 20–23%. The nominal 6 W is optimistic |
| Weight | About 0.45–0.55 kg, plus the cable | Typical density of glass plus an aluminium frame |

**Resulting design changes:**

| Item | v3 | v3.1 | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input TVS | SMAJ8.5A | **SMAJ10A** | Winter V<sub>oc</sub> may reach 9 V, above 8.5 V, so the TVS would conduct and heat up on sunny days |
| MPPT set point | About 5.5–5.8 V | **6.3 V**, with extra resistor footprints to change it to 6.0 V or 6.6 V | Between V<sub>mp</sub> at 25 °C and at 45 °C; with the chip now on our own board, we set the divider resistors ourselves |
| Fuse / PPTC | 1.5 A | Unchanged | I<sub>sc</sub> ≤ 0.9 A |
| Panel-side shunt resistor 0.05 Ω | — | Unchanged | 45 mV at 0.9 A, below the INA226's 81.9 mV range |
| Polarity | — | Designed as "red positive, blue negative" | The PMB-H input has reverse-polarity protection, so reversing the leads causes no damage; **you do not need to confirm it with a multimeter** |
| Cable | — | Keep the waterproof connector as the quick-disconnect; cut the panel end to about 30 cm; crimp a JST-XH 2P terminal on the PMB-H end | The 5 m cable is long and heavy |
| Backplate | — | Clamps onto the 15 mm thick frame; the junction box is within the frame, so the backplate can be flat | Photos |

## 4. Supercapacitors

Measured from photos at Ø10.2 × 25.3 mm, corresponding to the **standard Ø10 × 25 mm size**, with 5 mm lead pitch. The bag says "Kaimei / Zave", but Kaimei's official 2.7 V 10 F part is 12.5 × 21 mm, which does not match this one; it is probably a generic part from another manufacturer, which does not affect its use.

On the LIB: two in series lying flat, occupying about 25 × 21 mm; the third is a spare.
