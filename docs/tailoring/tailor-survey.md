# Who should make the vest: survey results

2026-10-12

## 0. Conclusion

**An ordinary alterations tailor cannot make this vest well. Go to a "bag / outdoor-gear sampling workshop" instead.**

The construction of this vest is closer to a backpack or a tactical vest than to ordinary clothing. It requires:

- 500D Cordura shell fabric, with 1000D reinforcement patches.
- Three rows of webbing, each row **bar-tacked** (needs a bar-tack machine).
- 5 m of **binding** around the neckline, armholes and hem (needs a binding folder / binder attachment).
- 5 **bellows pockets** with flaps and hook-and-loop closures.
- 2 **Ø7 brass grommets** (needs a press die).
- Two layers, shell + 3D spacer-mesh lining; side panels in four-way-stretch mesh.

Most alterations shops use domestic machines or single-needle lockstitch machines; sewing 500D plus multiple layers of webbing easily breaks needles and causes skipped stitches. Bag sampling rooms keep all this equipment as standard.

**Recommended two-step approach:**

| Step | Who | What | Rough cost / lead time (typical industry figures, confirm with a quote) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① Calico toile (recommended, not mandatory) | Any nearby tailor shop | Sew a body-only toile from cheap calico using the pattern; you try it on to check the shoulder and neck fit | CNY 100–200, 2–3 days |
| ② Final vest | Bag / outdoor-gear sampling workshop | Make the finished piece from the pattern and the requirements list in §3 of this document | Single-piece sample CNY 500–1500, 7–15 days |

Why do step ①: the shoulder and neck area is derived from body proportions, not measured directly from the scan (explained in guide §7). Testing in calico first is cheaper than reworking in Cordura.

### B. Changshu: a dense cluster of pattern-making and sampling workshops

- Changshu Garment City is one of the largest specialised garment markets in China, surrounded by many pattern-making and sampling services.
- A practitioner answering a Zhihu question about reliable garment pattern-making workshops noted that **there are many pattern-making workshops around Santang and Mocheng in Changshu**.
- Approach: before going, search Dianping, Douyin or Xiaohongshu in Chinese for "Changshu sampling bags" (pinyin: *Changshu dayang xiangbao*) or "Changshu pattern-making outdoor" (*Changshu daban huwai*), pick shops with real photos, and phone to ask the questions in §2 before visiting.

### C. Bag-industry clusters: Pinghu (Jiaxing), Shiling (Guangzhou)

- Pinghu is a bag-industry cluster; fabric, webbing, zips and buckles can all be sourced locally. Large factories usually have a sample room, but care more about order volume; **ask about single pieces**.
- Guangzhou Jiji Leather (ggpiju.com) makes tactical vests and has a "custom sampling" service page. But its product pages state an MOQ of ≥ 500 pieces, so it most likely will not take a single piece; only suitable for future volume production.

### D. Online made-to-drawing sampling (no legwork)

- Search Taobao and 1688 in Chinese; there are many shops for "bag sampling, made to your drawing" (pinyin: *xiangbao dayang laitu dingzhi*), "outdoor vest, sampled from your drawing" (*huwai beixin laitu dayang*) and "pattern-making and sampling, single piece" (*daban dayang danjian*).
- Prefer shops whose **profile says "sampling / custom" and that show real photos of samples**.
- Post the paper pattern files, or send the DXF directly.

### E. Local tailors (step ① calico toile only)

- Small local tailor shops are mostly inside residential neighbourhoods; older city-centre shopping streets often have a few long-established ones.
- Search Amap or Dianping in Chinese for "alterations" (pinyin: *gaiyi*) or "tailor" (*caifeng*) and pick one close to you.

**What I could not verify**:
- Amap, Baidu Maps and Dianping either require login or show a human-verification check, and I did not bypass them. So the current addresses and phone numbers of local shops could not be checked one by one.

## 2. Six questions to ask when phoning / messaging

1. **Do you take single-piece sampling?** Using the customer's own pattern (1:1 PDF and DXF available), what is the sampling fee?
2. Do you have a **bar-tack machine, binding folder, twin-needle or compound-feed machine**? Have you sewn 500D / 1000D Cordura?
3. Can you set **Ø7 brass grommets**?
4. Can you buy the materials on my behalf: 500D Cordura, 3D spacer-mesh lining, four-way-stretch mesh, 25 mm and 20 mm webbing, #5 separating zip? Or do I need to buy them and post them to you?
5. What is the lead time? Can I see photos of the part-finished piece before you finish it?
6. Can we sign a simple confidentiality note? This is a university research project; the pattern should not be shared.

## 3. Enquiry message ready to copy and send

> Hello, I am a university student making a custom vest for research, **only 1 piece**.
> I have a complete pattern: 1:1 plotter PDF and DXF, including 10 shell pieces, 10 lining pieces and pocket accessories.
> Materials: 500D Cordura shell + 3D spacer-mesh lining, four-way-stretch side panels, #5 separating zip at the front opening.
> Construction: three rows of 25 mm webbing on the back with bar tacks, 5 bellows pockets (with flaps and hook-and-loop), 25 mm binding on neckline, armholes and hem, 2 Ø7 brass grommets, and several elastic retaining loops on the lining.
> I would like to ask: ① do you take single-piece sampling, and roughly what are the cost and lead time? ② can you source the fabrics for me? ③ if convenient, I can send you the pattern and construction notes to look at. Thank you!
