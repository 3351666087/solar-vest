# nRF52832 pin budget - SolarBand V3-F

Generated by `tools/pin_budget.py` from `src/mcu_rf.py` and the built netlists (v3f and v3f_fldnp agree). nRF52832-CIAA WLCSP-50 only. P0.22-P0.31 are 'low drive, low frequency I/O only' (PS v1.4 Tables 5 and 6, p.17): only static or slow signals are on them.

WLCSP ring: 'outer' balls are reached on the top layer; 'inner' balls need a via-in-pad (JLC 6-layer: via 0.15 / 0.25 mm, POFV). Escaping the inner balls is a pass item of V3-G1 (BASELINE 18).

V3-F: 15 GPIO signals on outer balls, 17 on inner balls. Fixed-function inner balls: DCC (B6), VSS (C4, C5, E3, F5), VSS_PA (D2), N.C. (C3).

Spare GPIO: none (BASELINE 6: no pin is left; P0.24 becomes free if the LED chain goes).
