Solar Vest Explorer
===================

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Safari (double-click works; no internet needed
except for the fonts). Best on a laptop or projector at 1280 px wide or more.

Scenes (top bar)
  Worn               the vest on the body, energy and data flowing in real time
  Inside the boards  PMB-H and LIB as assembled by JLCPCB, every key chip named
  Bench rig          the tilt stand used for the outdoor tests

Mouse
  drag = orbit, scroll = zoom, right-drag = pan
  click any part or label = what it is and which problem it solves
  hover a part = its place in the flow graph lights up

Keys
  P            start / stop the guided presentation (11 steps)
  → / Space    next step      ←  previous step      Esc  exit / deselect
  X            X-ray (see-through shells)
  E            explode / assemble
  L            labels on / off
  F            show / hide the power & data flow graph
  1 – 9        camera views listed under the top bar

Operating modes (left panel): Full sun, Cloudy, Office light, Night, Cable pulled.
The numbers come from the CN3791 board simulation at a 3.70 V battery.
